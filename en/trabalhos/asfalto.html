Synopsis

Pavement Song is the result of an immersion in the city where I was born and lived my entire life. I positioned myself as a foreigner and sought to find an imaginary country, ending up in an Eastern reality while trying to depict the melancholy of no longer feeling at home and wanting to leave. The indecipherable Chinese immigrants of the city center were the right people for this portrait, and their collaboration and participation in the composition of this story was essential. Deep down, we are all very similar.
