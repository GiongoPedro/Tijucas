About the Festival

Metrô - Brazilian University Film Festival emerged in its first edition in 2017, with the desire to act as a catalyst for cinema in Curitiba and Brazil, following a continuity that pays homage to the defunct Putz festival, which also took place in Curitiba and had its last edition in 2009. Our main objectives are the need to encourage film students to have direct contact with university cinema currently produced in Brazil and the desire to stimulate and empower university production.

In addition to the screenings at the Cinemateca de Curitiba, the festival also organizes MetrôLAB, a consulting platform for young short filmmakers to have their projects mentored by industry professionals, roundtables on public-oriented topics, and a university jury.

Together with production company O Quadro, Tijucas is one of the creators and producers of the festival.
