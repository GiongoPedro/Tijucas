Imaginary Soundtracks
Original Soundtrack / Sound
Released February 28, 2020
All songs by Pedro Giongo • recorded in Recife / Brazil

Diary-album of the month preceding the arrival of the Coronavirus in Brazil. Inspired by Kraftwerk's Tour de France Soundtracks, for each new news, a new song was created.

Coronavirus OST

These 13 tracks became an album and are part of the Imaginary Soundtracks project.
