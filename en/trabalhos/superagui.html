Synopsis

On a mythical island on the southern coast of Brazil, we follow a 70-year-old fisherman who has not yet been able to retire. Martelo is a lighthouse man, and on the opening day of the fishing season, under the winter fog, he points his light towards Superagüi. We compiled a wishlist and combined it with the wishes of Martelo and other island residents, which speak volumes about the present moment: singing a traditional song, fishing for shrimp, going on a treasure hunt, improving working conditions, retiring with dignity, returning the island's life to how it used to be. The sea whispers at dawn: I am sleeping, you cannot wake me.
