Synopsis

the winds blow, it is the camera that flies low in a swoop and aims at the atomic city — a kamikaze and desiring mission without a destination, the plane eye captures frame by frame these crazy times, days of risk, FATAL October, there is no life to lose!

Photographed between October 28 and November 02, 2022, in Recife and Olinda.
