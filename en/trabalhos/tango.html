Synopsis

In a distant village, a mystical potato announces the restart of a new cycle. After a long drought, the population mobilizes for the traditional sacrifice ritual of Tango in the city center. During the celebration, the waters of the Aiatak River return, and with them, the life that resists until the arrival of a new annunciation. Inspired by the short story "A Hunger Artist" by Franz Kafka, "Tango" is a dive into human nature and its contradictions.

About the animation

Tango is an animated allegory that portrays our current times. This is the second animation from Estúdio Tijucas, and was produced between August 2014 and April 2016. Using the cut-out technique—stop-motion animation made with paper puppets—Tango is an entirely handcrafted film, with sets and puppets painted in watercolor, and scenes photographed frame by frame.
